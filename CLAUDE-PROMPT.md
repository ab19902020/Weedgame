# Green County — build the Berkshire open-world Android game

Act as lead gameplay engineer, technical artist and Android developer. Work in ab19902020/Weedgame from current main. Read the source, docs/CITY-DESIGN.md and the concept art, then run the existing APK or browser build and capture the Woodley starting scene as a baseline. Implement changes in the repository, test them through the normal player controls, and deliver a signed installable development APK. Continue through the milestones below, keeping a working build at each one.

## The experience to build
Cal and Leon Ward start with one Woodley garage, two friends and a battered hatchback. Berkshire is open from minute one. Build a fictional cannabis empire through growing, strain crosses, edibles, street orders, crew, contested blocks, outdoor land, warehouses and bulk deliveries. The player should recognise the journey from the shops and junctions before reading the map. Aim for the grounded UK feel, physical interactions and city activity of a PS3-era crime game. Use original or licensed assets. Preserve the brothers, existing saves, application ID, touch controls and offline play.

## 1. Solid 3D streets and a reliable renderer
Use Three.js. Pin a compatible version, bundle dependencies locally and implement WebGPU with an Android-tested WebGL fallback. Detect capabilities at runtime, handle renderer initialisation and device loss, and make both paths render the same playable scene. Display a recoverable error if startup fails. Log the active backend and frame timings in a developer panel. Separate fixed-step simulation, persistent world state and rendering.

Build every mapped building as a closed 3D mesh with a roof and a solid collision footprint. Handle concave outlines, courtyards and multipart footprints. Use a character capsule, sliding collision, swept vehicle collision and a camera that avoids walls. Derive rooflines, storeys and façade bays from surveyed or mapped data where available. Use plausible authored variations where data is missing. Model doors, reveals, sills, gutters, chimneys, shop signs and lit windows where the player can approach them. Use tileable brick, stone, render, glass and slate materials at consistent real-world scales. Give the garage, spray shop and owned facilities usable interiors.

Author Woodley, Cemetery Junction and Reading town centre first. Use reference images with recorded provenance and map footprints for Cemetery Arch, its flanking lodges and the Town Hall clock tower. Preserve Earley, Winnersh, Bracknell and the A329/A329(M) corridor. Check bridges, underpasses, slip roads and route connectivity. Keep road names, minimap, full map, waypoints and a route along drivable roads.

## 2. Vehicles and people with continuity
Give each vehicle a stable ID, model, paint, position, heading, velocity, condition, occupants and upgrades. Stream its graphics independently of its state. A parked or abandoned vehicle must still be there after walking away, swapping brothers, returning or reloading. Moving traffic should enter and leave view along connected UK lanes. Spawn distant traffic out of sight and avoid visible popping at cell boundaries. Implement junction priority, signals, queues, braking and recovery from blocked roads. Let players approach eligible vehicles, open the door, get in, accelerate, steer, brake, reverse, collide, park and get out safely. Let the garage change paint, wheels, lights, handling and cargo capacity, with visible or measurable results.

Give NPCs pavement routes, crossings, destinations, idle activities and reactions to nearby vehicles and disturbances. Crew can follow on foot, board available seats and leave with their brother. Use original rigged characters with walk, run, idle, enter/exit and interaction animations. Wardrobe choices must survive character swaps and reloads.

## 3. Police, rivals and consequences
Build fictional arcade systems for police patrols, witnessed incidents, escalating heat, pursuit, search, loss of contact and arrest. Support surrender, fines or cargo confiscation and a clear return to play. Add fictional weapon inventory, equip/holster, game damage, cooldowns, hit feedback, NPC reactions and recovery. Make confrontations playable through movement and choices. Rival activity should change the ownership and income of specific blocks. Show the consequences in the street, phone and territory map.

## 4. Give progression a physical presence
Start with one bench and a cheap lamp. Make each purchased light, watering system, rack and processing station appear where the player installed it. Let players create, name, harvest and sell fictional strains with inherited game traits and changing buyer demand. Link stock, staff, wages, vehicle storage, deliveries and warehouse capacity. Cal and Leon share the business while retaining individual position, outfit and current activity. Drive the first story chapter through phone calls with subtitles and stable audio IDs for future Fish Audio or ElevenLabs clips.

## Acceptance run and delivery
Play this sequence using touchscreen controls: leave the garage, walk into a wall and slide along it, enter the hatchback, drive a named route through a junction, deliver an order, park, swap brothers, upgrade the garage, return to the first brother and find the same car. Reload and verify money, crop, vehicle, crew and territory. Trigger a rival incident, see NPC and police reactions, and finish the encounter without restarting. Cross several streaming boundaries while watching traffic. Test a fresh save and a migrated v0.5 save, background/resume, WebGPU and fallback. Add focused regression tests for the failures you find.

Target a sustained 30 fps on an Honor Magic 6 Pro. Budget draw calls, texture memory, actors and streaming work. Use instancing, LODs and lower-cost distant simulation. Report measured frame times and test hardware; explicitly identify anything untested. Provide actual gameplay captures of the same locations before and after, build commands, asset credits, release notes, commit SHA and APK link. Keep signing keys and voice credentials outside Git.

First deliver the Woodley-to-Earley trip with solid buildings and a persistent, fully driveable car. Then continue through the remaining systems. If work spans sessions, commit the tested milestone and record the exact next task and known defects so another developer can resume.
