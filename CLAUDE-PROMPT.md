# Green County — build the living Berkshire sandbox first

Work from current main in ab19902020/Weedgame. Run the Android prototype and inspect its world import, renderer, actors, save and controls. Implement a freely explorable 1:1 Berkshire world in Three.js, with WebGPU and a tested WebGL fallback. The first launch puts the player straight into Woodley with control of a brother. Disable automatic mission calls, objectives, deliveries, sales, story triggers, empire progression and mission HUD in sandbox mode, including on existing saves. Preserve prior saves. The cannabis-empire game will sit on this world later.

## Geography and architecture
One world unit equals one metre. Cover Reading town centre, Cemetery Junction, Earley, Woodley, Winnersh and Bracknell town centre, their surrounding neighbourhoods, and the full connecting A329 and A329(M) routes. Define a continuous coverage polygon and audit it against the current corridor extracts. Import the residential streets between landmarks. Keep real coordinates, distances, junction geometry, gradients and relative elevations. Use a documented projected coordinate system, terrain and local rendering origins.

Aim to represent every house, building, road and traffic signal within that boundary. Import licensable mapping, terrain and building data. Supplement with permitted reference photography and local surveys where available. Track source, date, coverage and confidence by feature. Distinguish measured geometry, reference-modelled details and inferred details. Missing façade photos, signal timings or building heights belong in the coverage report. Never invent accuracy percentages.

Construct individual closed meshes from building footprints, including concavities, courtyards, attached terraces, garages and outbuildings. Add accurate heights and roof forms where documented. Model doors, bay windows, sills, chimneys, gutters, signs, boundary walls, gardens, hedges and driveways according to the property type and reference evidence. Build physically scaled brick, stone, glass, render, slate and asphalt materials. Give buildings, kerbs, walls, bridges and street furniture collision appropriate to walking and driving. Hand-author the Cemetery Junction arch and lodges, Reading Town Hall, Woodley shopping precinct and recognisable approaches to each town centre. Test views from real public vantage points.

Build roads as connected surfaces with left-hand lanes, turning lanes, slip roads, roundabouts, pavements, crossings, stop lines, markings, verges, bus stops, signs, streetlights and signal heads. Keep flyovers and underpasses on separate levels. Link signals to actual approaches and pedestrian crossings. Use documented phases where available and identify simulated timings. Full map and minimap must display road names, heading, both brothers and parked vehicles. All districts are reachable from the start.

## Exactly two playable people
Cal and Leon Ward are the only selectable characters. Give each a persistent outfit, location, heading and vehicle occupancy. Switch directly between the two wherever they are. Return to the same physical spot and situation after switching or reloading. Everyone else, including police and friends, is an NPC. Support third-person walking, running, camera orbit and entering, driving, parking and exiting vehicles using landscape touch controls.

## People, vehicles and police
Give pedestrian NPCs pavement and crossing navigation, destinations, time-of-day routines, walking groups, bus-stop queues, conversations, idle animations and varied appearances. Make them wait for traffic, avoid obstacles and react to horns, collisions, nearby disturbances and rain. Tune density by location and time: a Woodley residential close at dawn should feel different from central Reading on a Saturday afternoon.

Give vehicles stable IDs and persistent state independent of rendering. Simulate connected journeys, UK lane discipline, yielding, junction queues, signal compliance and recovery from blocked lanes. Include ordinary cars, vans and buses with appropriate scale and sound. Keep parked vehicles where they were left. Stream distant actors at reduced simulation cost and avoid visible pop-in or U-turns caused by a chunk boundary.

Make police part of the everyday simulation: visible foot and vehicle patrols, routine driving, and dispatch to witnessed simulated incidents such as a traffic collision. Model witnesses, responders, arrival, scene attendance, pedestrian reactions and eventual return to patrol. If the player causes a disturbance, provide proportionate fictional reactions, an intelligible stop or pursuit state and a clean resolution that returns control. Ordinary exploration should continue uninterrupted. Police encounters arise from the simulation and never become scripted missions.

## Weather, sky, light and sound
Build one coherent weather clock for the whole map. Blend clear skies, broken cloud, full overcast, drizzle, heavy rain, mist and clearing conditions over time. Give clouds visible depth, multiple scales, wind-driven motion and a continuous horizon. Use scalable volumetric clouds on capable devices and animated layered cloud shading at lower quality. Light the clouds from the same sun and moon as the streets. Passing clouds should soften sunlight and move shadows across buildings.

Rain accumulates gradually on roofs and roads. Show wet asphalt, restrained reflections, puddles and drying after a shower. Keep rain out of covered spaces. Wind should move foliage and drive rain consistently. Dawn, dusk and night change sky colour, exposure, streetlights, shop windows, vehicle lights and pedestrian density. Add spatial traffic noise, footsteps by surface, local voices, wind in trees, rainfall and shelter muffling. Save world time and weather through brother swaps, district streaming and relaunch. Provide developer controls for time and weather so these behaviours are testable.

## Delivery and acceptance
Implement the connected geospatial foundation first. Bring Woodley and Cemetery Junction to reference quality as early benchmarks, then apply the pipeline and finish coverage across the requested area. Stream world tiles, use mesh instancing and LODs, and keep simulation independent of draw distance. Bundle the required runtime assets for Android. Target sustained 30 fps on Honor Magic 6 Pro and record actual hardware, backend, frame time, memory and stalls during travel.

Test with normal touch inputs: launch into free roam; walk around a solid, identifiable building; cross at a working signal; watch a pedestrian and a bus complete a junction; park and switch brothers; return and find the car; drive across town boundaries and beneath a flyover; watch clouds move, rain start and roads dry; observe a police response and its resolution; close and resume. Check that no objective or mission appears. Compare georeferenced landmarks, route lengths, building footprints and signal coverage against the cited inputs. Deliver committed code, a signed development APK, actual gameplay captures, an asset/source register and a concise list of missing or estimated details. If delivery spans sessions, preserve a playable build and record exactly which geographic areas and systems still need work.
